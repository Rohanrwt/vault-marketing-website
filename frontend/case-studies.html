Auto Detailing Business — Calgary, AB
Meta Ads Local SEO Calgary
−55%
Cost per lead

The Problem

A Calgary auto detailing business was running Meta Ads independently. Leads were coming in, but at a cost that made profitability difficult. The ads were broad-targeted, the creative wasn't specific to the detailing market, and there was no local SEO presence to support organic lead flow.

What We Did

Rebuilt the Meta Ads campaign from scratch — tighter audience targeting, creative focused on before/after and specific service types. Built out Google Business Profile and local citations. Created a landing page built around the exact intent of someone searching "auto detailer Calgary."

The Outcome

Cost per lead dropped 55% within 90 days. Organic visibility increased across Calgary and surrounding areas. The business went from relying entirely on paid traffic to having a mixed inbound channel with compounding SEO value.

The Problem

New auto detailing business in Gurugram with no existing digital presence. Primary goal was to generate qualified leads at a cost that made the unit economics work — ideally under ₹200/lead in a competitive local market.

What We Did

Built a Meta Ads campaign targeting Gurugram and surrounding areas. Led with a specific offer (introductory pricing) and used video creatives showing the service in action. A/B tested audiences, ad formats, and landing page variations weekly.

The Outcome

Achieved consistent lead cost under ₹100 — well below the initial target. The campaign scaled to produce a predictable daily lead volume the business could staff around, effectively removing uncertainty from their growth planning.

Revenue generated

The Problem

A resort in Rishikesh was overly dependent on OTA platforms for bookings — paying high commissions, with no direct booking channel. The goal was to shift the revenue mix toward direct bookings and reduce OTA dependency.

What We Did

Built a direct booking funnel with a conversion-focused website, Meta and Google campaigns targeting intent-heavy travel audiences, and an SEO strategy around high-intent hospitality keywords for the Rishikesh market.

The Outcome

Generated ₹10Cr+ in total revenue through the direct booking channel. Direct booking share increased significantly, reducing OTA commission dependency and improving overall margin on bookings.

Real Estate Agency — Dehradun, India
Meta Ads Real Estate India
₹100
Cost per qualified lead

The Problem

Real estate agency in Dehradun spending heavily on ads with inconsistent lead quality. High volume, low intent — leads that weren't serious buyers, wasting sales team time and blurring actual campaign performance.

What We Did

Restructured the campaign to optimise for qualified lead quality rather than volume. Introduced pre-qualification in the lead form, tightened audience targeting to serious property buyers, and rebuilt ad creative around specific listings and investment angles.

The Outcome

Achieved sub-₹100 cost per qualified lead. More importantly, lead-to-meeting conversion rate improved substantially — the sales team was spending time on people who were actually ready to buy, not just curious.
