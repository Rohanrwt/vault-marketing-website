How we work

A simple, no-surprises
process.

We don’t disappear after onboarding. Every step is transparent, every deadline is communicated, and every decision is explained.

1.

Free strategy call

30 minutes. We learn your business, audit your current presence, and identify the fastest path to more leads—no pitch deck, just a real conversation.

2.

Custom growth plan

You get a specific plan: which channels, what budget, what KPIs, and a realistic timeline. No vague promises—just numbers we both agree on before work starts.

3.

Build & launch

Campaigns, content, or pages are built and launched within the agreed timeline. You review everything before it goes live.

4.

Optimize & scale

Weekly optimizations. Monthly reviews with plain-English reporting. When something works, we scale it. When it doesn’t, we fix it—and tell you why.

We’re not a generic agency that services every city. We understand Calgary’s market dynamics, seasonal patterns, and the specific way local businesses win trust. That context is worth more than any tool or template.

Month-to-month by default. We believe the only contract worth having is one where you want to renew. If we’re not delivering, you shouldn’t be trapped. That pressure keeps us sharp every single month.

You work directly with the person who built the strategy, not a junior account manager who joined three weeks ago. That means decisions get made faster, nothing falls through cracks, and you always know exactly who to call.
