## Case Study 1: Real Estate — ₹10 Crore in Plot Sales

**Tags:** Real Estate, Plot Sales, Meta Ads, Lead Generation, Dehradun, India

### Customer Profile
- **Industry:** Real Estate (Plot Sales)
- **Location:** Doiwala, Dehradun
- **Buyer Role:** Developer / Promoter

### Challenge
Developer had plots to sell in Doiwala, Dehradun but was relying on brokers and word-of-mouth. No digital marketing. No lead generation system. Competitors were running ads and capturing the market.

### What We Did
- Launched Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) targeting potential plot buyers within a defined radius
- Built a mobile-first landing page with lead capture
- Tested 4–5 creative variations in the first week
- Set up WhatsApp follow-up for every lead

### Results

| Metric | Number |
|--------|--------|
| Revenue Generated | ₹10 Crore |
| Total Leads | 200+ |
| Cost Per Lead | <₹200 |
| Ad Budget Spent | <₹50,000 |
| Timeline | 1–2 months |
| Industry Avg CPL | ₹800–₹2,000 |

### Pull Quote
> *"We were getting leads at ₹800–₹1,200 from other agencies. Vault brought it down to under ₹200 and the lead quality was actually better. ₹3 Crore in sales in just two months."*

### When to Use This
- Prospect is a **Real Estate developer** selling plots, apartments, or villas
- They're concerned about **lead cost**
- They've had **bad experiences with agencies** before
- They need proof that **Meta Ads work for Real Estate**

## Case Study 2: Hospitality — 1000+ Direct Bookings

**Tags:** Hospitality, Resort, Direct Bookings, OTA Elimination, Meta Ads, Reels, India

### Customer Profile
- **Industry:** Hospitality (Premium Boutique Resort)
- **Client:** Naqsh Resort
- **Buyer Role:** Resort Owner / GM

### Challenge
Resort was dependent on OTAs (MakeMyTrip, Booking.com) for bookings, losing 15–20% commission on every reservation. No direct booking strategy. No social media presence driving bookings.

### What We Did
- Full digital management: Meta Ads + Social Content (Reels, photography)
- Campaign designed to drive direct bookings and eliminate OTA dependency
- Created cinematic Reels and visual content that showcased the resort experience
- WhatsApp marketing for repeat guests and enquiry follow-ups

### Results

| Metric | Number |
|--------|--------|
| Direct Bookings | 1000+ |
| OTA Commission Saved | 15–20% per booking |
| Total Ad Spend | ₹1–3 Lakhs |
| Content Produced | Ongoing Reels + Photography |

### Pull Quote
> *"The direct bookings started coming in within the first two weeks. We eliminated OTA commissions completely on those reservations. The Reels they created made the resort look like a destination."*

### When to Use This
- Prospect is a **hotel, resort, or homestay** owner
- They're frustrated by **OTA commissions** eating margins
- They want **direct bookings** they control
- They need **content + ads** together

## Case Study 3: Local SEO — Page 1 Google Rankings

**Tags:** Local SEO, Google Rankings, Car Detailing, Ottawa, Canada, International

### Customer Profile
- **Industry:** Auto Detailing / Local Services
- **Client:** Glam Detailing
- **Location:** Ottawa, Canada
- **Buyer Role:** Business Owner

### Challenge
New car detailing business in Ottawa with no online visibility. Competing against established detailers who already dominated Google search results. Needed to be found by people searching for detailing services in Ottawa.

### What We Did
- Comprehensive local SEO strategy targeting high-intent keywords
- Google Business Profile optimization
- On-page SEO and content strategy
- Local citation building and link building

### Results

| Metric | Number |
|--------|--------|
| Google Ranking | Page 1 for high-intent keywords |
| Booking Impact | "Bookings like crazy" — Owner's words |
| Organic Traffic | Significant increase |
| Sales Channel | Search-to-booking with zero ad spend |

### Pull Quote
> *"I was getting bookings like crazy, not even talking to nobody. People just searched the keywords, found me on 1st or 2nd page, and they booked. The SEO completely changed my business."*

### When to Use This
- Prospect wants **SEO** in addition to ads
- They're a **local service business** (even outside India)
- They want proof that Vault can do **more than just Meta Ads**
- They want **organic traffic** that doesn't require ongoing ad spend
