# Discovery Call / Strategy Call — Talk Track

> **Duration:** 25–30 minutes
> **Goal:** Understand their situation, demonstrate expertise, qualify the lead, and book a follow-up where you present a proposal.
> **Rule:** Talk 30%, listen 70%. Ask questions, take notes, reflect back.

---

## 1. Opening (2 min)

> "Hey [Name], thanks for getting on the call. Quick agenda — I'd love to spend 10 minutes understanding what you're doing right now with marketing, then I'll share some ideas based on what I've seen work for similar [developers/resorts]. Sound good?"

**Why this works:** Sets expectations, positions you as the expert ("what I've seen work"), and gives them a reason to share openly.

---

## 2. Discovery Questions (12–15 min)

### Situation (understand where they are)

- "Tell me about your project — what are you selling right now?"
- "How many [plots/rooms/units] are you trying to move, and what's the timeline?"
- "How are you getting leads today? What's working?"

### Problem (uncover the pain)

- "What's your current cost per lead? Do you know?"
- "When leads come in, what happens next — who follows up, and how fast?"
- "Have you worked with a marketing agency before? How did that go?"

### Impact (make the pain real)

- "If nothing changes in the next 3 months, what does that look like for you?"
- "How many leads per month would change the game for your business?"

### Budget & Decision (qualify)

- "What are you spending on marketing right now — roughly?"
- "If I showed you a plan that could deliver [X leads/month], who else would need to see it before you go ahead?"

### Key things to listen for:
- **"We tried ads but they didn't work"** — Probe: what did the agency actually do? Usually creative/targeting was bad.
- **"We get leads but they don't convert"** — Follow-up speed issue. Your WhatsApp automation solves this.
- **"We don't have budget"** — They do if the ROI is clear. Use the ₹50K → ₹10Cr proof point.
- **"We need more walk-ins/bookings"** — They want end results, not leads. Position accordingly.

---

## 3. Present Your Approach (8–10 min)

> "Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd do if this were my project."

### For Real Estate:

> "We'd start with Meta Ads — Facebook and Instagram — targeting people in [city/radius] who match a buyer profile. The first week we test 4–5 different ad creatives to find what works. Within 7 days you'll see your first leads.
>
> Every lead gets an automated WhatsApp follow-up within 60 seconds — name, greeting, property details. No lead goes cold.
>
> We did exactly this for a developer in Dehradun. ₹50K ad budget, generated ₹10 Crore in plot sales. Leads came in at under ₹200 each — the industry average is ₹800–₹2,000."

### For Hospitality:

> "Right now, most of your bookings probably come through MakeMyTrip or Booking.com — and they take 15–20% commission on every reservation. That's ₹1,500–₹3,000 per booking just gone.
>
> We'd run Meta Ads targeting people who've shown interest in [destination/experience type], drive them to a landing page with a direct booking option, and follow up on WhatsApp.
>
> We did this for Naqsh Resort — generated 1000+ direct bookings. Total ad spend: ₹1–3 Lakhs. They kept every rupee of commission."

---

## 4. Handle Objections (if they come up)

See **03-objection-handling.md** for the full list. The top 3 you'll hear:

| They Say | You Say |
|----------|---------|
| "How much does this cost?" | "It depends on the scope, but let me put together a proposal with exact numbers after this call. I'll also include projected lead volume so you can see the ROI before committing." |
| "We tried ads, didn't work." | "That's actually the most common thing I hear. Usually the issue is targeting, creative, or follow-up speed — not ads as a channel. Can I ask what the agency was doing specifically?" |
| "Let me think about it." | "Totally fair. What's the one thing that would make this a clear yes or no for you? I'd rather address that now than leave it hanging." |

---

## 5. Close / Next Step (3 min)

> "Here's what I'd suggest — I'll put together a short proposal with exactly what we'd do, estimated lead volume, and pricing. I can have it to you by [day]. If it makes sense, we go live within a week. If not, no hard feelings. Sound fair?"

**Book the follow-up call on the spot:**

> "When's a good time to walk you through the proposal? I've got [Day] afternoon or [Day] morning."

**After the call:**
1. WhatsApp them the one-pager (01-one-pager.md) within 10 minutes
2. Send the proposal within 24–48 hours (04-proposal-template.md)
3. Follow up on WhatsApp if no response within 3 days

---

## Call Debrief Checklist

After every discovery call, note:

- [ ] What are they selling? (Property type / hotel / resort)
- [ ] What's the timeline? (Urgent = high priority)
- [ ] Current marketing spend?
- [ ] Biggest pain point?
- [ ] Who makes the decision?
- [ ] Follow-up date booked?
- [ ] One-pager sent?
